# What We Sow demo storyboard

The final production storyboard is
[Build the case](projects/what-we-sow/STORYBOARD.md), with measured framing and
motion in [SPEC.md](projects/what-we-sow/SPEC.md).

The user's final direction replaces the earlier narration draft: make the
one-minute film visual, use very few words, and show real gameplay and its
consequences. The cut has no PiP or voiceover. It uses actual game captures
from b716c41, real council arguments and choices, and a matched comparison of
retained fields versus fields taken for the reservoir, including compensation.
