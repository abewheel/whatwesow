# Judge film

Audience: Principled Business Summit hackathon judges. Summit thesis: “Capitalism's next 250 years won't be defended in a panel. Build the case.”

What We Sow makes the moral case for capitalism and equips players to make winning moral arguments. Fun comes first. Show an inviting farm built through work, then a moral argument with visible consequences for that work.

Current direction: a faster 60-second film. Rough land fades into small, medium and large beautiful farms. The male farmer keeps working in these takes. No apple tree. The talk track appears as captions; no spoken voice or PiP. The actual start-menu landscape closes the film.
