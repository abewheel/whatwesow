# Farm state atlas

`farm-states.png` contains six equal frames in three columns and two rows, matching the camera and palette of `farm-atlas.png`.

| Frame | Visual |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Farmhouse construction |
| 1 | Barn construction |
| 2 | Apple tree after picking |
| 3 | Well construction |
| 4 | Shed construction |
| 5 | Sapling |

Generated from the existing farm atlas as a style reference. The transparent backgrounds and isolated frames allow the renderer to use the artwork directly, without opacity effects or scaffold overlays. Construction and upgrade states use the variant corresponding to their finished building silhouette. Tree ripeness is separate from season and persists in saves.
