# Painted ground and border artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. These are static assets shipped with the game; generation is not part of gameplay.

- `ground-materials.png`: opaque square atlas, grass / cultivated soil on the first row, woodland floor / gravel on the second. Read quadrant dimensions from the source. `src/game/terrain.ts` color-matches each material for all four seasons, bakes contiguous 128px samples into 128 × 64 diamonds, and displays them at 64 × 32. Transparent gutters prevent neighboring atlas frames from bleeding. Soil beds add an inset edge and furrows. Tile phases and broad tonal changes depend on world coordinates, so terrain does not shuffle during camera movement. Cache each seasonal atlas once.
- `border-details-seasons.png`: transparent atlas, spring / summer / fall / winter rows, with wildflowers, fern and berry undergrowth, boulders and a fallen branch, and cattails in the four columns. The source has uneven gutters and a wider rock column; `src/game/border-artwork.ts` owns the frame rectangles. Preserve complete silhouettes and aspect ratios. Anchors sit at the bottom of each frame, outside the buildable estate.

## Ground prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production game ground material atlas, square image exactly 1024 by 1024, precisely four equal 512 by 512 square quadrants in a 2 by 2 grid, no gutters. Each quadrant is a seamless repeating TOP-DOWN FLAT surface texture, no isometric perspective (the engine projects it), no objects with height, no lighting gradient. Top left: lush meadow grass, short tiny layered blades, moss and clover texture, earthy olive and fresh sage greens, low contrast mottling with no flowers. Top right: rich warm brown freshly cultivated soil, granular earth, small irregular clods, tiny pale stones, no furrows (engine draws furrows). Bottom left: woodland floor, mossy dark green and umber leaf litter, small twigs, leaf fragments, fine fern impressions. Bottom right: warm gravel country path, varied small rounded tan/grey stones embedded in sandy earth. Style: richly detailed hand painted pixel-style cozy farming game materials, warm upper-left tiny highlights and fine dark crevices, match detailed painted farm buildings and foliage, readable texture when reduced dramatically. Uniform texture density, every quadrant filled fully to its edges, seamless tileable edges for each quadrant. No frames, lines, grid marks, borders, text, crops, buildings, cast shadows, scenic composition, sky, water, large rocks, or transparency.

## Border detail prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent game scenery sprite atlas, square 4-column 4-row grid with exactly sixteen isolated sprites. Each equal square cell has generous transparent gutters, entire sprite contained within central 80% width and central 65% height, no overlaps. Every sprite viewed in the same fixed 2:1 isometric camera, grounded low on its cell at 80% height. Rows are seasons: row 1 spring, row 2 summer, row 3 autumn, row 4 snowy winter. Columns are objects: column 1 a small low patch of wildflowers, clover and grasses (spring white daisies and pale pink flowers, summer lavender and gold, autumn seedheads and russet low plants, winter frosted stems with no blooms); column 2 a low woodland undergrowth cluster with ferns and a rounded berry shrub (lush greens then autumn gold and winter snow, never a tall tree); column 3 a low natural group of two irregular mossy grey boulders with a small old fallen branch (seasonal moss and snow); column 4 a narrow reed and cattail clump at a few river pebbles (fresh spring shoots then summer seedheads then autumn gold and winter frost). Richly detailed hand-painted pixel-style cozy farming game artwork, sculpted fine material detail, warm upper-left light, natural irregular silhouettes, restrained small ground contact shadows. Match warm detailed isometric farm cottages and woodland sprites. Sprite sheets only, transparent everywhere between plants and stones, no colored rectangular ground patches, no water, no terrain tiles, no scene backdrop, no UI, text, labels, borders, gridlines, pots, fences, buildings, large trees, or characters.
